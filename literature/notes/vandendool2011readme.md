## Contribution

*   **Research problem:** not stated (documentation page).
*   **Method:** Describes OCN maps: last 10 (T) / 15 (P) years averaged minus a 30-year climatology; operational skill mask and amplitude reduction.
*   **Data or materials:** 102 "super" climate divisions.
*   **Main result:** not stated.

## Quotes

1.  "Shown is the last 10 (T), 15(P) years averaged minus a 30 year climatology for each of 12 seasons." (page body)
    *   Supports: CPC OCN uses 10-yr windows for temperature and 15-yr windows for precipitation.
2.  "operationally we have a skill mask, i.e. where a-priori correlation is less than 0.3 no forecast is shown." (page body)
    *   Supports: operational OCN is withheld where a-priori correlation is below 0.3.
3.  "the amplitude of the operational OCN is reduced by a factor that relates to the a-priori skill so as to minimize RMS error." (page body)
    *   Supports: operational OCN anomalies are shrunk toward climatology in proportion to a-priori skill to minimize RMS error.
