# mascioli2017timing

*   **Citation:** Mascioli, N. R., Previdi, M., Fiore, A. M., & Ting, M. (2017). Timing and seasonality of the United States 'warming hole'. Environmental Research Letters 12(3), 034008.
*   **Identifier:** DOI 10.1088/1748-9326/aa5ef4; OpenAlex W2586358822 — https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/aa5ef4
*   **Retrieved:** 2026-11-09
*   **Access:** full text (`sources/mascioli2017timing.pdf`, `sources/mascioli2017timing.txt`)
*   **Type:** peer-reviewed article
*   **Relevance:** Defines and dates the U.S. warming hole; trends depend strongly on period, season and region.

## Contribution

*   **Research problem:** Roles of anthropogenic forcing and internal variability in the U.S. warming hole.
*   **Method:** Examine observed seasonal temperature trends over all periods of at least 10 years in 1901-2015; attribution to aerosols, NAO, PDO.
*   **Data or materials:** Observed U.S. temperatures 1901-2015; model experiments (details in paper).
*   **Main result:** Warming hole reflects both aerosol forcing and internal variability, with drivers varying by season, region and period.
