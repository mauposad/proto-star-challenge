## Contribution

*   **Research problem:** Impact of urbanization on USHCN temperature trends.
*   **Method:** Urban/rural classification by four proxies; paired and gridded trend comparisons; evaluation of homogenization with rural-only/urban-only reference series.
*   **Data or materials:** USHCN; U.S. Cooperative Observer network.
*   **Main result:** Urbanization accounts for 14-21% of the rise in unadjusted minimum temperature since 1895 and 6-9% since 1960; USHCN v2 homogenization largely removes it after ~1930.

## Quotes

1.  "Based on these classifications, urbanization accounts for 14% to 21% of the rise in unadjusted minimum temperatures since 1895 and 6% to 9% since 1960." (abstract)
    *   Supports: magnitude of the urban share in unadjusted Tmin trends.
2.  "The USHCN‐Version 2 homogenization process effectively removes this urban signal such that it becomes insignificant during the last 50‐80 years. In contrast, prior to 1930, only about half of the urban signal is removed." (abstract)
    *   Supports: homogenization removes most of the urban signal after ~1930.
