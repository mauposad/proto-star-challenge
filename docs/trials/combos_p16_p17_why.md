## Mechanism

**Claim.** An equal-weight mean beats its best member only when the members' errors are diverse enough that averaging cancels more error than it adds by pulling toward the weaker members. Here the four CDD members share most of their error (the same year-to-year weather noise), so the averaging gain is small, while the pooled ridge is clearly better than the other three. The mean therefore lands between the ridge and the average member: better than `current_best`, worse than `hybrid_ridge_all_cdd`. Estimated (inverse-MSE) weights cannot fix this because the members' hindcast MSEs are close, so the weights stay near equal (the P17 puzzle in its benign form).

**Check (CDD, years 4-7, pooled over folds).**

- Mean pairwise correlation of member errors: 0.962. Of what the members add beyond the 10-year mean: 0.518. The members do disagree about the signal, but that disagreement is small next to the shared noise.
- Spread (ambiguity) is 2.6 % of the average member MSE: that is all the squared error the equal mean can remove.
- The ridge's MAE is 2.5 % below the average member's (150.5 vs 154.3); the equal mean is at 152.3, between them, so the mean loses to the ridge.
- Temperature, same window: the 10-year mean member has MAE 1.222, the equal mean 1.242 and DeepAR 1.198; EWMA and ARIMA are worse, so combining temperature costs skill against `current_best`.
- Scores agree: years 4-7 `combo_mean` 4.91, `combo_inv_mse` 4.92, `combo_mean_cdd_only` 5.37, `hybrid_ridge_all_cdd` 5.54, `current_best` 4.68.

Verdict: the check **supports** the mechanism. The inverse-MSE weights themselves were not logged; "near equal" is inferred from the near-identical scores (TBD: log them).

## Caveats

- Variants: 8 candidates and 2 seed replicas, fixed in the config before the first run, plus 3 reproduction rows and 3 references. Nothing was added after seeing results.
- Seeds: DeepAR uses 3 internal seeds (20170411, +1000, +2000) and one replica set (seed 7). The pooled ridge, EWMA and ARIMA are deterministic.
- Three origins only; differences under about 1 point are within noise. The best candidate is picked from 8, so its score is optimistic, and it still loses.
- `hybrid_ridge_all_cdd` itself was the best of 15 round-1 variants, so the reference is optimistic too: a fair comparison would discount it, which this trial does not do.
- The members were chosen in round 1 because they beat the 10-year mean on CDD in the same folds, so all combinations inherit that selection.
- Trimmed mean: a symmetric trimmed mean of 4 members equals their median, so `combo_trimmed` drops the member farthest from the median instead (a deviation).
