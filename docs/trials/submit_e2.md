# Trial: submit_e2

The pooled-trend candidate plus E2: the city-specific part of each city's recent temperature and CDD anomaly (the last 15 valid years minus the 30-year mean, less the cross-city mean) is damped by a coefficient fitted on in-fold pseudo-origins (lambda about 0.6-0.8). Precipitation and hottest day stay on the 30-year mean.

## Forecasters

| forecaster | definition |
|---|---|
| thirty_year_mean | Mean of valid years in the 30 calendar years to the cutoff (reference) |
| current_best | Per metric: annual_avg_temp=ten_year_mean, total_precip_in=thirty_year_mean, hottest_day_temp=thirty_year_mean, cdd=ten_year_mean |
| trend_level10_15_sensitivity | Mean of last-[10, 15]-valid-year levels plus a non-negative pooled 40-year trend for temperature and CDD; CDD trend = pooled temperature trend x each city's CDD per degree; 30-year mean for precipitation and hottest day |
| e2_temp_city_shrink | Pooled-trend candidate (levels 10/15, pooled 40-year slope, CDD per degree) with the city-specific part of the recent 15-year temperature and CDD anomaly damped by an in-fold coefficient fitted on 30 pseudo-origins |
