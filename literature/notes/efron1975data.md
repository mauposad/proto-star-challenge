*   **Relevance:** James-Stein / empirical Bayes shrinkage of many parallel means; background for partial pooling of per-city estimates.

## Contribution

*   **Research problem:** Apply Stein's estimator in an empirical Bayes context to real data.
*   **Method:** Stein's rule and generalizations.
*   **Data or materials:** Baseball averages, toxoplasmosis prevalence (spelled "toxomosis" in the retrieved abstract), simulated chi-square test sizes.
*   **Main result:** Mean square error less than half that of the sample mean in each example.

## Quotes

1.  "In 1961, James and Stein exhibited an estimator of the mean of a multivariate normal distribution having uniformly lower mean squared error than the sample mean." (abstract)
    *   Supports: James-Stein estimator dominates the sample mean in MSE.
2.  "This estimator is reviewed briefly in an empirical Bayes context." (abstract)
    *   Supports: the paper frames Stein's estimator as empirical Bayes.
3.  "In each of these examples, the mean square error of these rules is less than half that of the sample mean." (abstract)
    *   Supports: large MSE reductions in the three applications.
