# proietti2025estimation

*   **Citation:** Proietti, T., & Giovannelli, A. (2025). On the Estimation of Climate Normals and Anomalies. arXiv:2511.05071v2.
*   **Identifier:** arXiv 2511.05071 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05071
*   **Retrieved:** 2026-11-09
*   **Access:** full text (`sources/proietti2025estimation.pdf`, `sources/proietti2025estimation.txt`)
*   **Type:** preprint (not peer reviewed)
*   **Relevance:** V01 in papers.csv. Confirms authors: a regularized real-time local trigonometric regression filter for climate normals under trends; applied to ENSO-region series, not U.S. stations.

## Contribution

*   **Research problem:** Standard 30-yr normals may be unsuitable under nonstationarity; choose bandwidth and account for trends.
*   **Method:** Regularized real-time filter based on local trigonometric regression; seasonal kernels.
*   **Data or materials:** Niño 3.4 sea surface temperature; equatorial/tropical Pacific wind series.
*   **Main result:** not stated in abstract beyond "illustrates the relevance of our proposal".

## Quotes

1.  "Climate normals are currently estimated by simple arithmetic averages calculated over the most recent 30-year period ending in a year divisible by 10. The suitability of the standard methodology has been questioned in the context of a changing climate, characterized by nonstationary conditions." (abstract)
    *   Supports: the standard 30-yr normal is questioned under nonstationarity.
2.  "The literature has focused on the choice of the bandwidth and the ability to account for trends induced by climate change." (abstract)
    *   Supports: prior work centers on window length and trend handling.
3.  "The paper contributes to the literature by proposing a regularized real time filter based on local trigonometric regression, optimizing the estimation bias-variance trade-off in the presence of climate change, and by introducing a class of seasonal kernels enhancing the localization of the estimates of climate normals." (abstract)
    *   Supports: the method is a regularized real-time local trigonometric regression filter targeting the bias-variance trade-off.

## Not Covered

*   U.S. station data, annual totals or extremes, degree days, or multi-year-ahead MAE evaluation. Whether its application data extend past 2018 was not checked (the method itself uses only past data of the series).
